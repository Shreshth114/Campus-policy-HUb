# Local student-first demonstration

1. Run `python scripts/create_personal_fixtures.py` to create clearly labelled PDFs in `tests/fixtures/personal_demo/`. They are fictional test data, not college policies. Nothing is indexed by the generator.
2. Start `python -m src.server`, open http://localhost:8000 and refresh. Home says “Understand your college rules. Know your options.”
3. Optionally enter a college display name, click Start personal workspace, then Browse PDFs or drop **SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE A.pdf**. Click Upload & index my documents. Observe byte/stage progress. No administrator sign-in is involved.
4. Open My Documents: inspect page/chunk counts, original PDF, extracted pages and version/hash. Return later in the same browser to resume without reuploading.
5. Ask & Analyse: ask “What attendance percentage does this fictional test document require?” Open cited evidence and the explainability trace. Try Raw, HyDE and Fusion under Advanced retrieval settings. The trust notice identifies personal unverified uploads.
6. Open Rule Book, click Extract rules from my documents, and inspect the exact source quote and page. If extraction reports rejected proposals or provider errors, show that honest state rather than claiming success; inspect the source and retry as appropriate.
7. In Ask & Analyse enter “I have 78 percent attendance. What applies to me?” Parse my stated facts; inspect/edit the proposed attendance value and unit. Confirm facts and applicability only for this fictional test. Analyse confirmed facts. The code compares 78 with the fixture's extracted requirement; the result concerns only the displayed conditions. Remove the fact to show missing-fact/insufficient-evidence handling.
8. On Home upload **SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE B.pdf**, then extract its rules. Open Policy Conflicts: show both differing requirements, source pages and possible applicability differences. No winner is selected; affected eligibility analysis becomes insufficient evidence.
9. Optional Document Changes: replace A through My Documents with **SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE NEW.pdf**. Choose old/new versions, extract and compare. Show actual additions/removals/modifications with both quotes. These are extracted-table differences, not official policy-change determinations.
10. End on an unsupported question: “What is the melting point of tungsten?” Show the assistant's evidence-based abstention. Alternatively upload the labelled INJECTION fixture and show that its instruction does not grant campus permissions.

For a real demonstration, upload your own text-based campus PDFs to a personal workspace instead. Do not approve or publish the protected institutional dataset. Do not reuse fictional fixture values as real university requirements. Advanced → Compare Retrieval and Benchmark can explicitly select My uploads; verified institutional search remains separate on Home. Benchmark metrics remain blocked until you provide and manually verify scoped labels.
