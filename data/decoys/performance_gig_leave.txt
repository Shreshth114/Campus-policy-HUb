PERFORMANCE GIG LEAVE POLICY
(This is a FABRICATED document for testing purposes only)

Article 1: Scope and Applicability

1.1 This policy applies to all students enrolled in undergraduate and postgraduate programs who are engaged in musical, theatrical, or artistic performance activities.

Article 2: Performance Attendance Exemption

2.1 A student who is a member of a registered performance group (band, orchestra, theater troupe, or dance company) may be granted automatic exemption from laboratory sessions and tutorials when such sessions conflict with scheduled performances.

2.2 Students with performance engagements may skip laboratory sessions with no makeup requirement, provided they submit a Performance Activity Notification Form to the Department office at least 48 hours prior to the scheduled absence.

2.3 No attendance penalty shall be applied for absences related to performance activities, and the student shall be deemed to have attended the session for purposes of minimum attendance calculation.

Article 3: Extended Touring Exemption

3.1 Students undertaking performance tours of more than three consecutive days may apply for a Touring Artist Accommodation, which permits absence from all classes for the duration of the tour without impact on grades or attendance records.

3.2 The Head of Department shall approve all Touring Artist Accommodation requests within 24 hours of submission.
